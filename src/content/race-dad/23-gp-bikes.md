If you race motorcycles in Ontario, there is a very high probability that you are intimately familiar with GP Bikes in Whitby. For the racing community, it is essentially the candy store. 

When Jäger started pushing harder on the Ohvale and outgrowing his early gear, we needed serious protection. You can’t just order custom race leathers off Amazon and hope they fit properly when a kid is sliding across the asphalt at 100km/h. 

![A young racer in full leathers being helped on an Ohvale in the paddock](/images/race-dad/gp-bikes/young-racer-full-leathers-being-helped.jpg)

That is where having a massive, dedicated superstore locally makes all the difference. GP Bikes was instrumental in helping make Jäger's Team Canada race suit happen. The team there actually understands what road racers need. 

Whether it is a last-minute scramble to find the right set of knee sliders on a Thursday night before a race weekend, or getting fitted for a brand-new helmet to make sure the cheek pads are snug, they are the go-to resource. 

For a Race Dad, having that kind of inventory just down the road from Oshawa is a massive relief. 

It is also a testament to how deep the motorcycle culture runs in this area. You walk in, and you are surrounded by people who understand the obsession. Big shoutout to the crew at GP Bikes for supporting the minimoto scene and making sure Jäger is protected when the green flag drops.
