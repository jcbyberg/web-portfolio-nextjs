Everything in the FIM MotoMini Canada series builds to one singular date: November 26th. 

That is when the FIM MotoMini World Final takes place in Valencia, Spain. 

For these kids, Valencia is the absolute pinnacle. It is where the national champions from the USA, Italy, Spain, Malaysia, and beyond all meet to prove who is the fastest mini rider on the planet. 

Getting there is a massive logistical and financial hurdle, but the chance to represent Canada on the world stage is the ultimate motivator. When you are testing in the grueling, 35-degree heat of August at Shannonville, Valencia is what keeps you pushing. 

Jäger raced the World Finals in 2025, and the experience of lining up against the European kids who practically live on these bikes is incredibly eye-opening. The pace is furious. 

But having that target on the calendar elevates the entire Canadian series. The kids know what they are fighting for, and as parents, we know exactly how high the bar is set. All roads lead to Spain.
