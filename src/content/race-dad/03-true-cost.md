"How much does it actually cost to get a kid into racing?" 

It’s the first question every parent asks when they see Jäger's #68 Ohvale on social media. The short answer is: it depends on how deep down the rabbit hole you want to go. 

If you are just dipping your toes in the water, the Super Sonic Road Race School (SSRS) "Arrive & Race" program is incredibly accessible. You show up, they provide the bike and the gear, and your kid gets world-class instruction in a safe environment. It’s the perfect, low-risk entry point.

But what happens when the bug bites? What happens when you commit to a full season in MiniSBK and FIM MotoMini Canada? 

That’s when the spreadsheet changes. You’re looking at buying an Ohvale GP-0 or GP-2. You’re factoring in the cost of PMT slick tires (and you will burn through them faster than you think), high-octane race fuel, and synthetic oil for changes after every race weekend. 

Then there are the entry fees for the national rounds, transponder rentals, and the inevitable crash damage—because bending a rearset or snapping a lever is just part of finding the limit. 

And don't forget the travel. Towing a trailer to Shannonville and Lombardy all summer means a lot of gas money, campsite fees, and paddock food. 

It adds up. But honestly? When you compare it to the cost of elite travel hockey or competitive dance, it is surprisingly competitive. 

More importantly, you are buying an experience you can’t get anywhere else. The time spent wrenching together in the garage, the lessons in resilience when they crash and get back up, and the memories made around the campfire with the paddock family? 

You can't put a price tag on that.
