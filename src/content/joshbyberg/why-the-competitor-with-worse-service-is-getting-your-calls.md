The Mobile UX & Speed Angle
*Use this if the intent is to highlight friction in the user journey.*

It is a frustrating reality in the trades: you know you do better work, you show up on time, and your pricing is fair. Yet, that other guy across town keeps getting the calls you should be getting. 

It isn't because they are better at the job. It is because their phone number is two seconds easier to find on a mobile screen. 

When a homeowner in Oshawa needs a contractor, they don't open a laptop and read ten pages of your company history. They pull out their phone, search, and click. If your website takes 6 seconds to load, requires them to zoom in to read the text, and hides the phone number on a separate "Contact" page, you have already lost. 

Your competitor is winning because their site loads instantly and features a massive "Tap to Call" button fixed to the bottom of the screen. We build $500 flat-rate sites that do exactly this. Stop losing jobs to worse contractors just because your website is hard to use.
