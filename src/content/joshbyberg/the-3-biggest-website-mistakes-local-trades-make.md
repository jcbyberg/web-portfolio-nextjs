The Lost Leads Angle (Focus on ROI)
*Use this if the intent is to highlight how much money bad design is costing them.*

If you run a local trade in Durham Region, you don't need a website that wins design awards. You need a website that rings the phone. Unfortunately, most contractors are losing half their digital leads because of three easily avoidable mistakes.

**1. No "Tap-to-Call" Button:** 80% of your traffic is on mobile. If a homeowner has a burst pipe and they have to pinch, zoom, and copy-paste your number into their dialer, they will just call the next guy on Google.
**2. Hiding Your Service Area:** "Serving the GTA" is too broad. If you don't explicitly list Oshawa, Whitby, or Ajax on your site, Google won't show you to locals searching for "plumber near me."
**3. The Bloated Desktop Design:** Massive auto-playing videos and 10-page "About Us" stories slow down your site on mobile networks.
