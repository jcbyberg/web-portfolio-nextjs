The Scaling & Vulnerability Angle (Strategic)
*Use this if the intent is to appeal to trades owners looking to scale or hire more crews.*

"We don't need a website. We get all our work through referrals." 

In the trades, saying you are a "referral-only" business is a badge of honor. It means you do great work and people trust you. But as a business strategy, relying entirely on word-of-mouth is incredibly dangerous. 

When your entire pipeline depends on your past clients remembering to mention your name at a BBQ, you have zero control over your growth. You cannot scale a business on unpredictable favors. What happens when the local economy tightens? Or when you want to hire a second crew and need to double your lead volume overnight? 

A high-converting, mobile-first website is a referral engine that works 24/7. It allows you to turn the faucet on when you need work and control the exact type of jobs you bring in.
