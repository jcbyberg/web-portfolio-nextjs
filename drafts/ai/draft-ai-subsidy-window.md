## 3. Blog Body: Wording Options

### Option A: The Historical Cloud Analogy (Strategic & Analytical)
*Use this if the intent is to appeal to veteran tech founders who lived through the AWS/Cloud transition.*

If you were in tech around 2007 and 2008, you remember the "Cloud Push." Amazon, Google, and Microsoft were handing out massive subsidies, free credits, and slashed pricing to convince companies to migrate off on-premise servers. It was a gold rush of artificially cheap compute power designed to capture market share. 

Then, once everyone was locked in, the subsidies disappeared and the real pricing kicked in. 

We are living through the exact same phenomenon right now with Artificial Intelligence. That $250-ish Claude Max plan or the pennies you are paying per million tokens on the OpenAI API? It is heavily subsidized. You are getting access to thousands of dollars worth of enterprise-grade compute power at a fraction of the actual cost because the big players are fighting for dominance. 

But this window won't last forever. 

The smartest companies aren't just using cheap AI to write emails; they are using this subsidized window to build permanent infrastructure. At Whitespace, we are helping businesses use these cheap LLMs to write the hardcoded scripts, webhook integrations, and API pipelines that automate their operations. 

The strategy is simple: Use the AI while it's cheap to build the automation. Once the infrastructure is built and the code is written, your company runs on autopilot. When the AI price hikes inevitably arrive, you won't care—because your business is already automated, and you are no longer reliant on renting their intelligence. Get the work done now, and save thousands.

### Option B: The Urgency & Direct ROI Angle (Aggressive & Action-Oriented)
*Use this if the intent is to drive immediate action and highlight the financial risk of waiting.*

Right now, you are paying around $250 for a top-tier Claude or OpenAI plan that gives you access to intelligence that would have cost tens of thousands of dollars just two years ago. The AI labs are burning billions in venture capital to subsidize your subscription. 

It is the greatest arbitrage opportunity in modern business, and it is going to end. 

Just like the early days of AWS and cloud computing, these companies are keeping prices artificially low to capture the market. The moment the dust settles and a winner is declared, those API costs and subscription tiers are going to spike. If your entire business model relies on pinging an LLM every five minutes, your margins will evaporate overnight.

So, how do you exploit this window? You don't use AI as a crutch; you use it as a builder. 

Our focus right now is helping clients leverage this cheap, subsidized AI to write permanent code. We use it to build the custom API integrations, data pipelines, and hardcoded scripts that automate your daily operations. You use the AI to build the machine, so that when the AI gets expensive, you don't need it anymore. The machine just runs. 

Build your automation infrastructure today while the labor is effectively free. If you wait until the price hikes, you will be paying a premium just to survive.
