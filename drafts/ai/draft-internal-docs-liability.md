## 3. Blog Body: Wording Options

### Option A: The "Dead Data" Angle (Operational Efficiency)
*Use this if the intent is to highlight the uselessness of current SOPs and Google Drive structures.*

Every scaling company eventually runs into the same problem: You spend months writing detailed Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), onboarding guides, and brand guidelines. You put them in a Google Drive folder. And then nobody ever reads them. 

When a new employee has a question, they don't search through 50 pages of a PDF. They tap a senior manager on the shoulder and interrupt their workflow. 

Your internal documentation is a liability. It takes time to create, but provides zero operational leverage. 

The most impactful use of AI in business right now is fixing this exact problem. We build custom internal AI agents that ingest all of your "dead data"—your PDFs, Slack histories, and Notion pages—and turn them into an instant-answer operational brain. Instead of interrupting a manager, your team asks the AI: "What is our refund policy for enterprise clients?" and gets the exact answer, cited from page 42 of your employee handbook, in three seconds. 

### Option B: The Training & Onboarding Angle (HR/Scaling)
*Use this if the intent is to target companies hiring rapidly and struggling with onboarding times.*

How long does it take for a new hire to become profitable? 

In most agencies, the first three months are a massive drain on resources because senior staff spend hours every week answering basic questions. "Where is the client logo file?" "How do we format this report?" "What is our pricing for this tier?"

You can't scale efficiently if your senior talent is constantly acting as an internal help desk. 

This is where a custom AI Agent becomes your most valuable employee. We connect Large Language Models directly to your company's proprietary documentation. When you hire someone new, their first line of support isn't your lead developer; it is the internal AI. It knows every process, every guideline, and every past project, and it never gets tired of answering questions. Stop wasting senior talent on junior training.
