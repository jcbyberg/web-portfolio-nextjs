### Option A: The Lost Leads Angle (Focus on ROI)
*Use this if the intent is to highlight how much money bad design is costing them.*

If you run a local trade in Durham Region, you don't need a website that wins design awards. You need a website that rings the phone. Unfortunately, most contractors are losing half their digital leads because of three easily avoidable mistakes.

**1. No "Tap-to-Call" Button:** 80% of your traffic is on mobile. If a homeowner has a burst pipe and they have to pinch, zoom, and copy-paste your number into their dialer, they will just call the next guy on Google.
**2. Hiding Your Service Area:** "Serving the GTA" is too broad. If you don't explicitly list Oshawa, Whitby, or Ajax on your site, Google won't show you to locals searching for "plumber near me."
**3. The Bloated Desktop Design:** Massive auto-playing videos and 10-page "About Us" stories slow down your site on mobile networks. 

We strip away the bloat. For $500, we build fast, mobile-first sites that turn local searches into booked jobs.

### Option B: The Customer Frustration Angle (Focus on UX)
*Use this if the intent is to build empathy for the customer's journey.*

Put yourself in the shoes of a homeowner in Whitby whose furnace just broke in February. They pull out their phone, search for a local HVAC tech, and click your website. What happens next?

If they are met with a slow-loading template from 2015, tiny text they can't read, and a generic contact form instead of a direct phone number, their frustration shifts from the broken furnace to your business. 

The biggest mistake local trades make is treating their website like a digital brochure instead of an emergency hotline. Your customers want three things immediately: proof you are local, proof you are credible, and a giant button to call you right now. 

Stop overcomplicating it. We build clean, lightning-fast sites designed specifically for mobile users who need your services immediately.
