*Use this if the intent is to explain why Google prefers their competitors.*

You have been serving Durham Region for 15 years, but when someone searches for your exact service in Whitby, your competitor shows up first on Google. Why? 

It isn't magic, and they didn't pay Google off. They simply have a website that speaks Google's language.

We don't just build sites that look good on phones; we hardcode the local SEO data right into the foundation so that you show up where you belong: in front of local customers.
