# Draft: Stop Trying to Sell "Exposure" to Sponsors

## 1. Post Metadata & Strategy
- **Target Audience:** Race teams, independent riders, track operators.
- **Goal:** Educate racers on selling digital assets rather than trackside views, driving demand for custom media kits and team websites.
- **Required Action:** Post to the `race-dad` collection.

## 3. Blog Body: Wording Options

### Option A: The Content Creation Angle (Marketing Focus)
*Use this if the intent is to show racers how to act as a marketing agency for their sponsors.*

The most common mistake amateur racers make when pitching local businesses is trying to sell "exposure." You tell the local plumber that there will be 5,000 fans at the track who might see their logo on your lower fairing as you blur past at 120 mph. 

The local plumber does not care. That is a terrible return on investment for their marketing dollars. 

If you want to secure funding, you need to stop selling trackside exposure and start selling digital assets. Local businesses desperately need high-quality content for their own social media. When you pitch them, your offer should be: "We will provide you with 10 high-resolution, professionally edited photos and 2 video reels of your branding in action at the track, completely cleared for you to use in your own Facebook ads and Instagram feed."

You are no longer asking for a handout; you are acting as a highly specialized content creation agency for their business. At RaceDad, we help teams build the professional liveries and digital infrastructure needed to deliver this exact B2B value. 

### Option B: The Digital Asset Angle (B2B Focus)
*Use this if the intent is to highlight the value of a dedicated team website for sponsors.*

Why do companies sponsor race teams? Because racing is inherently cool, and they want their brand associated with it. But a logo on a bike isn't enough in the digital age. 

When you ask a company for a check, you need to give them a digital asset in return. If you don't have a professional team website, you have nothing to offer. 

Imagine changing your pitch from, "Put your sticker on my bike" to, "We will feature your company on the official Partners page of our team website, providing a high-quality backlink for your SEO and driving our dedicated fan traffic directly to your storefront." 

That is a business proposition. It is measurable, it lives forever (unlike a race weekend), and it provides actual digital value. We build custom websites for motorsport teams so you can stop selling vague "exposure" and start selling concrete digital real estate.
